How to Delete Your Account

This guide explains how to delete your UnnatiRupeeCredit account and the personal data associated with it, directly inside the Application.

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Overview

You can delete your account and clear your data at any time from within the Application. The option is available while you are signed in, and it is located in the Settings screen of the third tab at the bottom of the home screen.

The deletion takes effect immediately in the Application: your account is closed, your data is cleared from your device, and you are returned to the login screen.

Steps to Delete Your Account

1
Sign in to the Application Open the Application and sign in with your mobile number and verification code. Account deletion is only available while you are logged in.
2
Open the third tab On the home screen, tap the third tab at the bottom of the screen — the rightmost tab, labelled Mine.
3
Tap "Settings" In the menu on the Mine page, tap Settings.
4
Tap "Delete Account" On the Settings screen, tap Delete Account.
5
Confirm the deletion A confirmation dialog appears asking whether you want to delete your account and clear all of your account data. Tap Yes to confirm. If you change your mind, tap No.
6
Done Your account data is cleared, the message "All your account data has been cleared" is shown, and you are returned to the login screen. Any remaining step is simply to uninstall the Application if you no longer wish to use it.
!

Important: Outstanding Loan Orders

If you have a loan order that has not been fully repaid, account deletion may fail on the server side. An active or unsettled order is tied to your account record, and the account cannot be fully closed while that record still needs to be retained.

If the deletion does not go through, please settle the outstanding amount first, then repeat the steps above. If it still fails after your loan is fully repaid, contact us at pixelnfft@gmail.com and we will help you complete the deletion.

What Is Deleted

Deleting your account clears the personal data associated with it:

  • Your account and login session
  • The basic profile and identity information you submitted
  • The reference contacts you provided
  • The device and technical information collected during your application
  • The data stored locally on your device by the Application

Some records may be retained where the law requires it, or for the periods described in our Privacy Policy — for example, records needed to comply with financial and anti-money-laundering obligations.

Cannot Sign In?

If you can no longer access your account — for example you changed your mobile number or cannot receive the verification code — you can request deletion by email. Send your request from the mobile number or email associated with your account, and the Service Provider will process it after verifying your identity.
